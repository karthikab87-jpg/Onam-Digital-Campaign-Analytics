🌼 Onam Digital Campaign Analytics – Power BI Work Sample
📌 Project Overview

This project was created as part of the Onam Tech Creation Challenge. I used a Google Ads campaign-performance dataset and adapted it into an Onam-themed digital campaign analytics use case.

The objective was to analyse campaign performance, understand sales and conversion behaviour, and present the results through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The original dataset contained digital advertising performance data. For the Onam-themed work sample, I created meaningful Onam campaign categories while retaining the underlying performance metrics.

📐 Measures / KPIs Used

1. Total Sales
Shows the overall revenue generated.

2. Total Cost
Shows the total advertising expenditure.

3. Clicks
Shows how many times users interacted with the advertisements.

4. Conversions
Shows the number of desired actions completed after interacting with the campaigns.

5. Conversion Rate

Conversions ÷ Clicks × 100

This helps understand how effectively clicks are converted into desired actions.

6. ROAS – Return on Ad Spend

Sales ÷ Advertising Cost

This indicates how much revenue is generated for every unit of advertising expenditure.

📌 Business Suggestions

Based on the dashboard analysis, businesses can:

Focus more budget on high-performing campaigns that generate better sales and ROAS.
Improve low-converting campaigns by reviewing their targeting, advertisements or landing pages.
Monitor conversion rate along with clicks, rather than judging campaign success only by traffic.
Compare cost versus sales before increasing campaign spending.
Monitor campaign performance over time and adjust the strategy during high-performing periods.
Use campaign-level analysis to make future festive marketing decisions.
